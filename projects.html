EDC - A Fermi Paradox Solution Hypothesis

The Evolutionary Dispersal Collapse (EDC) project is a computational simulation investigating whether intelligent civilizations could naturally evolve to stop expanding across the galaxy because the costs of interstellar travel outweigh the benefits of colonization. I learned how evolutionary pressures, resource availability, competition, and inherited behavioural traits can influence the long-term expansion of civilizations, potentially explaining why the galaxy isn't filled with extraterrestrial civilizations.
